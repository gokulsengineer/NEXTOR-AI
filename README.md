Your AI. Your Learning. Your Future.

NEXTOR AI is a learning platform concept I'm building for engineering students.

The idea is to bring learning, problem solving, exam preparation, career guidance and project support into one platform.

What I'm Exploring

- AI-powered learning
- Student-focused tools
- Engineering problem solving
- Tamil / Tanglish / Simple English learning support
- Career and skill guidance
- Project and hackathon support
- College learning features

Current Status

NEXTOR AI is currently under development.

NEXTOR AI is my attempt to turn that idea into a real product.
